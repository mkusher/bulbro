Skills (stats), levels and upgrades

How the Bulbro stat system works as of 28 September 2026, compared with Brotato's Upgrades. "Skills" here means the 16 primary stats plus 2 secondary stats every Bulbro has. Everything above Phases describes the current code.

Stats are stat points summed from a list of stat sources, and the combat formulas give the points their meaning. Damage, attack speed, crits, armor, dodge, life steal, HP regeneration, harvesting and range follow Brotato's formulas. Level-up upgrades and items do not exist yet. They are meant to plug in as new stat sources (see Phases).

How stats work

  1. Types. Stats = MainStats & SecondaryStats (web/src/bulbro/BulbroCharacter.ts). MainStats holds the 16 Brotato primary stats; SecondaryStats holds pickupRange and knockback.
  2. Base values. baseStats (web/src/game-formulas.ts): maxHp 10, speed 450, pickupRange 100, everything else 0.
  3. Stat points. Each stat is base + the sum of its points. The exceptions are speed and pickupRange, where points are a percentage of the base (450 × (1 + points/100)). The combat formulas decide what the other points mean; for example, 60 damage points means +60% damage. Max HP is at least 1.
  4. Stat sources. BulbroState.statSources: StatSource[], where each source is { id, kind, bonuses } and kind is "character" | "harvestingGrowth" | "upgrade" | "item" | "level". computeStats(sources) produces BulbroState.stats, and withStatSource(source) adds a source or replaces the one with the same id, then recomputes. Today only character and harvestingGrowth sources are created.
  5. Display. The shared percentageStats set decides which stats are shown with a % sign (speed, damage, attack speed, crit, life steal, dodge, luck, pickup range).
  6. Enemies use a separate EnemyStats shape with flat values scaled per wave (buildWaveStats). Their damage is flat damage, not a percentage.
  7. Network sync. Clients exchange events, not state, so every random outcome is rolled in an event generator and carried in the event. Crits are baked into the damage of the shot or strike. A dodged hit produces no event. Life steal produces a heal event. Luck decides the consumableId on enemyDied. Reducers stay deterministic.

Levels and experience

  • XP sources: each collected material gives +1 material and +1 XP regardless of harvesting, and harvesting gives XP at the end of a wave (BulbroState.gainMaterials).
  • Co-op shares every pickup (intended). materialCollected carries the collecting playerId, but every player gets +1 material and +1 XP. Players level at the same rate from pickups and differ only by their own harvesting. Covered by tests in BulbroState.spec.ts and waveState.spec.ts ("co-op shared pickups").
  • Curve (web/src/bulbro/Levels.ts): Bulbros start at level 0, and reaching level n takes (n+3)² more XP (16, 25, 36, …), as in Brotato. A level is reached exactly at its threshold.
  • State: BulbroState.level follows the XP (getLevelForExperience) and never goes down. The in-wave XP bar uses the same functions.
  • Rewards: none yet. There is no level-up event, no +1 max HP and no upgrade choice.

Current issues

  1. High Levels give nothing. Level and XP are tracked, but there is no +1 max HP and no upgrade choice. This is covered by Phase 2.
  2. Medium No feedback for crits, dodges or life steal. The mechanics work, but HitIndicators shows nothing special, so players can't tell their stats are working.
  3. Medium No stats panel during a run. BulbroStateStats shows live BulbroState.stats, but only stories use it. The character selector shows the character's stats only. After upgrades and items, the shop needs a live stats view.
  4. Medium Engineering has no effect, yet Medic (+3) and Cyborg (+5) have it. There are no structures.
  5. Medium Elemental damage barely matters. Only the laser gun scales with it (50%), and there are no elemental weapons or burning.
  6. Medium Luck only affects consumable drops. Material and crate drops and shop and upgrade rarity ignore it.
  7. Medium Balance is untested. With no free baseline regeneration, only Medic regenerates. Character points, weapon crit and scaling values, and armor extremes (King −40%, Berserker +33% damage taken) are first guesses.
  8. Low Harvesting in network games is applied in the tick reducer on the running→ended transition, so it should run once per state. This is covered by reducer tests but has not been verified in a live two-player game.
  9. Low Enemy stats reuse player stat names with different meanings. Enemy damage is flat, and enemy attackSpeed of 1–2 points is only 1–2% faster.
  10. Low HP regeneration heals in 1-second chunks.

Brotato upgrades (reference)

From the Brotato wiki: every level-up offers a choice of 4 upgrades, each raising one primary stat. Offered upgrades can be rerolled. Upgrades come in 4 tiers. Tier rarity depends on level and Luck in the same way as shop items. Some levels guarantee a tier:

  • Level 1: 100% Tier I
  • Level 5: 100% Tier II
  • Levels 10, 15, 20: 100% Tier III
  • Level 25 and every 5th level after that: 100% Tier IV

In Brotato, each level-up also grants +1 Max HP, and the upgrade choices appear at the end of the wave, before the shop, once per level gained.

What is missing compared to Brotato

Level-up flow

  • +1 Max HP per level gained.
  • An end-of-wave "Level up" screen before the shop: 4 choices, one pick per level gained, and a reroll whose price grows with the wave.
  • Upgrade tiers (I–IV), rarity rolls driven by level and Luck, and guaranteed tiers at levels 5/10/15/20/25+.
  • Co-op handling: each player picks their own upgrades, and the picks are synced as events.

Stat mechanics

  • Engineering and structures; elemental weapons and effects (burning).
  • Luck on material and crate drops, and on shop and upgrade rarity.

Secondary stats (item-only in Brotato)

Pickup range and knockback exist. Missing: XP gain %, consumable heal, materials-on-heal, piercing, bounce, explosion size/damage, free rerolls, shop price modifiers, enemy count/speed modifiers, harvesting growth modifiers, and structure stats.

Items

The shop only sells weapons (ShopItemsGenerator.ts). There is no passive item type, no item tiers, no item stacking or limits, and no trigger effects.

Phases

Phase 0: stat foundation — done

  • Stat points model, StatSource list, computeStats and withStatSource.
  • Removed the duplicate props.speed; Brotato XP curve starting at level 0; renamed getLevelForExperience; level kept in sync with XP.
  • WeaponStats with cooldown (renamed from weapon attackSpeed), crit chance and multiplier, and damage scaling.

Implementation note: characters were rebalanced into points (for example, Medic's regeneration went from 100 to 5), and each character's first source uses the id "character". Specs build BulbroState with statSources: [] and explicit stats, so calling withStatSource in those specs recomputes stats from base.

Phase 1: stats in gameplay — done (except engineering and full luck)

  • Damage pipeline (scaling, damage %, crit), Brotato attack speed, armor, dodge, life steal, HP regeneration, harvesting, half range for melee weapons, and luck on consumable drops.

Implementation note: random rolls take an injectable random argument and happen in event generators, not reducers. The free baseline regeneration was removed on purpose. Harvesting runs in the tick reducer only when a wave is survived.

Phase 2: level-up upgrades — next

  • Co-op pickups are shared, so players reach level-ups together; each player still picks their own upgrades.
  • An upgrades-definitions/ module with the 16 Brotato upgrades × 4 tiers (table above). Leave engineering out until structures exist.
  • +1 max HP per level as a level stat source, and pending upgrades counted from the levels gained during the wave.
  • A level-up screen between the wave end and the shop: seeded choices (like ShopItemsGenerator), tier rolls by level and luck, reroll cost, and upgradeSelected/upgradesRerolled events that add upgrade stat sources.
  • Show live stats in the shop and level-up screen (BulbroStateStats).

Phase 3: items

  • An item model (id, tier, basePrice, bonuses: StatBonus, optional limit and tags), sold in the shop next to weapons with prices from itemPrice. Each purchase adds an item stat source.
  • Start with pure stat items and trade-offs, then secondary-stat items, then trigger items.
  • Luck on shop rarity.

Later

  • Feedback for crits, dodges and life steal in HitIndicators.
  • Engineering with structures, and elemental weapons with burning.
  • A balance pass.
